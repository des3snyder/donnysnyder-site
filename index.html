Political Science

Donny Snyder

I am a PhD Candidate at the University of Massachusetts, Amherst, in the Department of Political Science, with a focus in American Politics and Quantitative Methods. Additionally, I serve as a Graduate Fellow at the UMass Amherst Poll.

My research interests lie in machine learning text-as-data methodologies, political psychology, and affective polarization. I am curious about the way that the structure of political information influences partisans' psychology and their perceptions of the political world. I am also interested in developing empirical interventions that may reduce affective polarization.

I am currently funded through the National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship Program (NSF GRFP). If you have any questions about my ongoing research, please send me an email at desnyder@umass.edu.
